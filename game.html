THE HUNT

You are a sperm whale. Your eyes are small and the squid are deep. Click, and the world answers: near things answer quickly, far things late. High voices are small or above you; low voices are large or below. A shimmer is a thousand fish; a warm thump is family; a breath of static, barely there — that is what you eat. Your own voice is silent in your ears; you hear only what comes back. Hold your creak on a contact and the buzz quickens as you close. Learn the sound of everything while the light lasts. Then dive.
